# November 3 consumer-adoption browser profiles

`canvas-profiles.json` preserves the five completed records pasted after the
November 3 manual run. The final three records are the ordinary `npm start`
observations compared in the November 3 section of `startup-and-canvas.md`.
The first two are retained browser history from November 2 and used different
instrumentation settings; they are not part of that three-run comparison.

The completed `mounted` record supplies Canvas duration. Screenshot timers were
captured before completion. Command totals can overlap and do not constitute
additive wall-time portions. These observations do not contain derived-registry
hit/miss or reconstruction counters, and do not establish causal savings.
